# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Householders and landlords with a pest problem (rats, mice, wasps, bed bugs…) looking for a local firm to call, usually on a phone, often in a hurry.
- Businesses (food premises, offices, landlords, facilities managers) looking for a local commercial pest contract.
- Independent pest control firms, who claim or submit a listing and can buy a Featured spot.

## Product Purpose
PestPro Index is a UK directory of independent, local pest control firms, organised by city and borough/town. A visitor finds the firms based in or covering their area and contacts them directly by phone or website. Success: the visitor reaches a local firm quickly and trusts the list.

## Positioning
We only list independent local pest controllers. National chains are not included. No lead fees, no commissions: visitors call firms directly. The directory order is by Google rating; Featured spots are paid, labelled, and kept separate from that order.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js App Router, Supabase `Providers` table, Tailwind CSS v4, deployed to Railway from `main`.
- All URLs, SEO titles and meta must be preserved.
- The local-only wording and the "no national chains" disclaimer must stay.
- Featured listings: currently Pest Away UK only; which firms are Featured is data, not design.
- Top-rated marker rule: Google rating 4.5+ from 30+ reviews; not paid for, cannot be bought.
- For Professionals page carries the Stripe payment links for Featured spots.

## Brand Commitments
Name: PestPro Index. Voice: plain, direct, local, factual. No sales language, no invented claims.

## Evidence on Hand
Firm data (name, postcode, phone, website, Google rating and review count, services) from Supabase. No testimonials, customer quotes, photography or press exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
1. Local first: every page should make clear the firms are near the visitor.
2. Honest ordering: paid placement is always labelled and never changes the rating order.
3. Call in one tap: contact details are the main action on mobile.
4. Domestic and commercial both matter.
